# Session 01 — Founding Document
*April 25, 2026*

## What we established
Designed the complete architecture for the Shared Brain — a human-AI 
cognitive partnership built on three layers:

- **Open Brain (Supabase)** — the vault, raw knowledge, source of truth
- **Notion** — operational workflow, tasks, client management
- **Obsidian Wiki** — compiled understanding, reflects how I think

Claude is the nervous system connecting all three.

## Three operating principles
1. **Lean contradiction resolution** — address errors the moment they 
surface, never let them compound
2. **Versioned thinking** — capture the arc of learning, not just the 
current state
3. **Building in public** — content creation and knowledge capture are 
the same motion

## Three capture modes
1. Auto-summarization of documents and media
2. Pure chat capture — conversation is the source
3. Doc-in-conversation — linked annotations saved beside source documents

## Key insights
- The Shared Brain is a record of how I think, not just what I know
- Every project lifecycle ends in a completion package that graduates 
to the vault
- This is the skeleton of a Kaizen-native AI-first consultancy
- The wiki isn't just compiled knowledge — it's compiled truth that 
can correct both human and AI
- Named "the Shared Brain" — not Open Brain — because it describes a 
relationship, not infrastructure

## Decisions
- Build on Supabase + Obsidian + Notion
- Start intentionally — add client files first, grow from there
- Nothing goes to the vault without a moment of intention
